Profile

I’m someone who has worn many hats in her career - from digital production, education, marketing, sales, to project management. In all of these roles, one simple truth stands out: rushing into “urgent” tasks without a plan wastes hours (for the team and client) and stresses everyone out. I’ve been there, and it’s never fun, no matter what kind of hat you are wearing.


For me, good planning means helping everyone stay focused and informed, creating breathing room to handle surprises without losing our minds. When projects hit bumps (and they will), clear communication and realistic expectations keep the team aligned and sane. That’s my work mantra.

I believe everyone’s background shapes how they approach work and solve problems. I’ve seen this firsthand on projects where some teammates jump straight into action while others prefer to plan every detail first. I’ve learned to recognize and respect these styles, helping the team find common ground instead of friction. I focus on understanding people, not just the tasks at hand, and believe diverse teams bring fresh perspectives that lead to better solutions and a more engaging work environment. That’s the kind of team I want to be part of and help build.


I like working with people who take ownership, follow through, and aren’t afraid to speak up when things just don’t feel right. That kind of honesty and responsibility lets us do great work without burning out. Plus, it turns "oh those busy days of being busy" into opportunities to actually grow.
